# GoreeCloud Advanced Download Manager — Competitive Objectives

This document records development objectives, not product rankings or claims of demonstrated superiority.

GoreeCloud Advanced Download Manager should aim to combine performance, transfer durability, automation, privacy, security, and cross-device orchestration without relying on a mandatory hosted control plane.

Objectives include intelligent parallel downloading that adapts rather than simply maximizing connection count; restart-safe and validator-safe transfer persistence; first-class Linux, Windows, and Android experiences built around one shared transfer model; transparent rules and post-processing; explicit bandwidth, storage, network, power, and scheduling policy; local-first privacy with user-controlled history and remote access; a reusable service/API for participating GoreeCloud applications; safe delegation to specialized services such as GoreeCloud Swarm; and high-quality Glaze UI accessibility.

Comparative performance or feature claims must be backed by repeatable benchmark or product evidence before publication.
